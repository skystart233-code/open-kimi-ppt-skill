# Rights-holder notice policy

PPTD Studio Skill respects copyright, trademark, privacy, and other rights.
The maintainers do not intentionally distribute third-party branded templates,
logos, or media without a documented license or other permission.

## Sending a notice

Open a GitHub issue at
<https://github.com/skystart233-code/pptd-studio-skill/issues/new> with a clear
title such as `Rights notice`. Do not include confidential personal information
in a public issue. If confidentiality is required, first ask in the issue for a
private contact channel.

Please include enough information to evaluate the request:

1. the rights holder and the reporter's authority to act;
2. the specific file, URL, mark, or material at issue;
3. the right claimed and, when available, registration or source information;
4. why the repository's use is believed to be unauthorized or misleading;
5. the requested remedy and a reliable reply channel; and
6. a good-faith statement that the information supplied is accurate.

## Review process

The maintainers will preserve relevant evidence, review the identified material,
and may temporarily disable or remove it while the claim is assessed. A
contributor who supplied the material may be asked for license or authorization
evidence. Restored material should include a documented rights basis.

This process does not determine legal liability and does not limit any rights or
remedies available under applicable law.
