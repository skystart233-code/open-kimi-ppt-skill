# Provenance and clean-brand boundary

This document separates required open-source attribution from product branding.
Historical names appear here only to identify source material accurately.

## Project identity

- Current project: **PPTD Studio Skill**
- Package and CLI: `pptd-studio-skill`
- Skill directory: `pptd-studio`
- Maintainer repository: <https://github.com/skystart233-code/pptd-studio-skill>

The project does not use a third-party vendor name or logo as its identity and
does not claim official compatibility certification, sponsorship, or endorsement.

development history; they are not current branding or a claim of affiliation.
